# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes employee attrition, workforce demographics, and job satisfaction to help HR teams understand why employees leave.

![HR Analytics Dashboard](images/hr_analytics_dashboard.png)

## Project Objective
- Measure overall employee attrition and identify high-risk groups
- Understand workforce composition by age, gender, and department
- Explore how job role, education field, and job satisfaction relate to attrition

## Dashboard Features
- Attrition rate by job role
- Employees by age group and gender
- Attrition by education field
- Attrition by age
- Department-wise employee distribution
- Job satisfaction rating by job role
- Slicers: Gender, Marital Status, Business Travel, Education Level

## Key Insights
- **Sales Representatives** have the highest attrition rate (39.76%), followed by Laboratory Technicians (23.94%) and Human Resources (23.08%).
- **Research Directors** have the lowest attrition rate (2.50%).
- Employees from **Life Sciences** and **Medical** backgrounds account for 152 of the 237 attrition cases.
- Most employees are aged **25–44**, and there are more male than female employees across age groups.
- Attrition peaks among employees in their **late 20s to early 30s** and declines after that.
- **R&D** is the largest department (65.37% of employees), followed by Sales (30.34%) and HR (4.29%).
